Manufactured Move

Two markets price the same index: a small one where trading actually moves the price, and a much larger options market that only follows along. Here's how you turn that gap into money — one step at a time — and what you'd walk away with.

STEP 1

Buy the index up

All morning, keep buying the index. Your own buying pushes it up — from 100 to about 102. The price is now high, and you're holding a lot of it.

STEP 2

Bet against it, big

At that peak, turn to the far larger options market: sell calls (expensive now) and buy puts (cheap now). A big bet that the index will fall — priced off the inflated 102.

STEP 3

Sell it back down

Now sell the index back off. It slides to 100. Your options bet, placed right at the top, is now deep in the money.

The result. Moving the index cost you about 40. The options bet earned about 10,079. You walk away with roughly +10,000 — over 250× what it cost to move the market. You paid a little in the small market and collected a lot in the big one. Steer the sliders above to change the trade and watch the number move.
